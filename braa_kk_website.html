About Us

Your Partner on the Building Site

"Braa KK is a supplier of Sand, Hardware, and Building materials offering a wide range of services including Sand supplies, Transportation, Demolition, and site clearance. We have the ability and resources to source and supply all quantities of sand materials."

Construction & Site Work

We specialize in building strong, beautiful houses for our community. Our team knows how to build safely and on time.
